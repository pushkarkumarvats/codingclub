About Solvothon

What is Solvothon?

Solvothon is IIT Guwahati's premier hackathon and problem-solving competition, bringing together the brightest minds to tackle challenging algorithmic problems and innovative coding challenges.

Who Can Participate?

Open to all students passionate about coding and problem-solving. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced coder, Solvothon has challenges for everyone!

Why Join?

Compete for exciting prizes, gain recognition, network with fellow coders, and enhance your problem-solving skills in a competitive environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any registration fee?

No, Solvothon 2025 is completely free to participate in. We believe in making quality coding competitions accessible to everyone.

Can I participate individually or do I need a team?

You can participate both individually or as a team of up to 3 members. Choose what works best for you!

What programming languages are allowed?

You can use any programming language of your choice including C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, and more.

Will there be mentors available during the event?

Yes! We'll have experienced mentors and industry experts available throughout the event to guide participants.

Is this an online or offline event?

Solvothon 2025 will be conducted in hybrid mode - both online and offline at IIT Guwahati campus.

How will winners be selected?

Winners will be selected based on problem-solving accuracy, code efficiency, and completion time.
